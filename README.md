# CVResumeBuilder

A modern, ATS-optimized Resume and Bio-Data builder designed to help job seekers bypass automated recruitment filters and land more interviews.

[![Live App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_App-Launch_Builder-2563eb?style=for-the-badge&logo=rocket&logoColor=white)](https://cvresumebuilder.web.app)

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters (The Problem It Solves)

Over **75% of resumes are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** before a human recruiter ever sees them. Resumes designed in tools like Canva or Word often use complex tables, floating text boxes, and flattened images that ATS software (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever) cannot parse.

### How CVResumeBuilder Solves This:
- **Guaranteed ATS Parsing:** Produces clean, single-stream vector PDFs where 100% of the text is machine-readable and selectable.
- **Job Description Keyword Matching:** Directly compare your resume against any target job description to pinpoint missing skills and increase your interview call rate.
- **Zero Formatting Frustration:** No more broken margins or shifting tables. Type your details and the system handles pixel-perfect alignment automatically.
- **Versatile for All Career Stages:** Covers modern tech developers, corporate executives, academic researchers, and entry-level freshers.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🎯 Real-Time ATS Score & Keyword Matcher
- **Job Match Percentage:** Paste any job circular to receive an instant match score (0–100%).
- **Missing Keyword Suggestions:** Identifies essential skills and industry keywords missing from your resume.
- **Action Verbs Library:** Built-in recommendations for high-impact action verbs to strengthen your work experience bullet points.
- **ATS Robot View:** Terminal inspection mode that reveals the exact raw text stream recruiters' ATS bots see.

### 2. 🎨 15 Industry-Tested Templates
- **Tailored Layouts:** Formats optimized for Software Engineers, Corporate Executives, Academics, Legal Professionals, and Creatives.
- **Classic Fresher Bio-Data:** Specialized format for Indian freshers and entry-level roles—includes boxed layout, personal particulars (DOB, Father's Name, Marital Status), tabular academic records, and formal declarations.
- **Seamless Switching:** Change templates anytime without re-typing or losing your data.

### 3. 📝 Complete Section Flexibility & Auto-Clean
- **Comprehensive Fields:** Personal Info, Professional Summary, Work Experience, Projects (with live demo and GitHub links), Education, Skills, Certifications, Languages, Achievements, Publications, and Custom Sections.
- **Smart Auto-Omit:** Any section or field left empty is automatically excluded from the final preview and PDF—no blank spaces or messy gaps.
- **Photo Upload:** Profile photo with automatic lossless compression (≤200KB) for crisp display without large file sizes.

### 4. 🔀 1-Tap & Drag Section Reordering
- Arrange sections in the order that best highlights your strengths.
- Full drag-and-drop on desktop, plus 1-tap **Move Up (↑)** and **Move Down (↓)** buttons optimized for mobile screens.

### 5. 📱 Real-Time A4 Live Preview
- True-to-life A4 paper preview updates instantly as you type.
- Responsive auto-fit scaling for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- Mobile toggle between full-page view (**Fit**) and comfortable reading zoom (**Read**).

### 6. 📄 Clean Vector PDF Export & 1-Click Backup
- **Direct PDF Export:** 1-click download of ATS-proof vector PDF ready for job applications.
- **Local JSON Backup:** Export your entire resume profile to a lightweight JSON file and restore it on any device at any time.

### 7. 📂 Multi-Resume Management
- Create, duplicate, and manage multiple resume versions tailored to different job applications from a single account.
